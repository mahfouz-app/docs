How Mahfouz stacks up

Mahfouz vs the rest.

Every one of these tools can hold your notes. The difference is what happens to them afterward: whether they stay plain files you own, or become records in someone else's database on someone else's server. Here's how they compare side by side.

Feature comparison

File format & ownership Plain .md files in a folder you choose. Proprietary blocks in Notion's database. Plain .md files in a local vault. Proprietary storage in Atlassian's cloud. An app-managed local database.
Where it runs Your computer or your browser. No server to run, ever. Notion's cloud. Your computer and phone. Confluence Cloud. Data Center closed to new customers in March 2026. Your desktop; sync needs AppFlowy Cloud or your server.

How Mahfouz stacks up

Mahfouz vs the rest.

Every one of these tools can hold your notes. The difference is what happens to them afterward — whether they stay plain files you own, or become records living inside someone else's database and someone else's server. Pick a comparison below.

Mahfouz vs Notion

Plain Markdown files you keep vs. proprietary blocks kept in Notion's cloud database.

Mahfouz vs Confluence

No hosting to run, ever, vs. a cloud subscription — with Confluence's self-hosted option closing to new customers.

Open source vs. open source — but only one of them needs no server, self-hosted or otherwise.

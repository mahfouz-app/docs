Mahfouz vs Notion

Mahfouz vs Notion

Notion organizes your work as blocks inside its own cloud database. Mahfouz keeps every note as a plain Markdown file on your computer, readable by any editor, script, or AI agent, with nothing to export because nothing was ever locked away.

Feature comparison

Compare Notion Mahfouz
File format & ownership Proprietary blocks stored in Notion's own database. Pages only exist inside Notion. Plain .md files in a folder you choose. Open, edit, or grep them with any tool.
Where it runs Notion's cloud. Requires an account. On your computer, or in your browser at web.mahfouz.app. No account needed, and Mahfouz has no cloud of its own and keeps no copy of your notes.
Version history 7 days on Free, 30 on Plus, 90 on Business; unlimited only on Enterprise. Full history in a real Git repo, kept as long as you keep it, not a vendor's retention window.
Offline use Partial, and only in the desktop and mobile apps. Free downloads the pages you choose; paid plans also download recent and favorite pages. Everything else needs a connection. Fully local-first. Works offline by default; there's no "download for offline" step.
Sync & collaboration Real-time, in Notion's hosted workspace. Built in, through any Git remote you choose. Everyone works from their own full copy, and track changes lets people suggest edits. There's no live multiplayer editing.
In the browser Yes. The web app is Notion's main client. The web app at web.mahfouz.app opens a folder on your computer or a GitHub repository, with no copy kept on Mahfouz's servers.
AI agents Notion's own AI agents are fully included only on Business and Enterprise. External agents such as Claude Code connect through Notion's official MCP server. Agents can read the files directly, and Claude Code, Codex, and other MCP clients can connect to Mahfouz, with per-vault access and edits that wait for your approval.
Databases Built in, with many view types. Built in. Each row is a note, with table, board, gallery, and calendar views.
Tasks To-dos, plus task databases with assignees, due dates, and reminders. Turn a checklist item into a task with a due date, an assignee, and a reminder, and see every task in a list or on a board.
Presentations Presentation mode (beta), on Plus and above. Present any note as slides styled by a vault-wide template, and export the deck to PDF or PowerPoint (desktop, Slidev plugin).
Publishing Notion Sites, from your workspace. Publish a note as a public page at a link you share, and keep it in step with the note.
Track changes Suggested edits you accept or reject, with comments. Built in. Suggest edits instead of making them, then accept or reject each one, with comments alongside.
Platforms Web, macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android. No Linux app. macOS and the web today. Windows and Linux are coming soon.
Price Free tier is limited; paid plans run from €9.50/member/month (Plus) to €19.50 (Business), billed per seat. Free. No seats. The only cost is whatever Git hosting you choose, if any.
Data export / lock-in Workspace export to HTML, Markdown, or CSV on every plan, but it's a one-time snapshot, not how the data normally lives. Nothing to unlock: notes are files. Export a note and its children to Markdown with their attachments, or to PDF.

Notion pricing and plan limits as published on notion.com in October 2026; check their pricing page for current figures.

Questions

Notion, answered.

Can I open my notes without Mahfouz?

Yes. Every note is a plain .md file. Open it in any text editor, script against it, or hand it to an AI agent; nothing is trapped in a proprietary format the way Notion's blocks are.

Does Mahfouz have real-time multiplayer editing like Notion?

No. Mahfouz is a local-first, single-writer editor. Collaboration happens through Git: push, pull, and merge like any other repo, rather than everyone typing into the same live document.

What does Mahfouz cost compared to Notion?

Mahfouz is free, with no seats to pay for. Notion's paid plans are billed per member per month, and page history beyond 90 days needs Enterprise.

Can I bring my Notion workspace into Mahfouz?

Yes. The Notion import plugin copies pages, databases, to-dos, comments, and files into a vault through Notion's API, so you don't export anything first. It runs in the desktop app.

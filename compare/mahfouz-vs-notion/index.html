Mahfouz vs Notion

Mahfouz vs Notion

Notion organizes your work as blocks inside its own cloud database. Mahfouz keeps every note as a plain Markdown file on your computer — readable by any editor, script, or AI agent, with nothing to export because nothing was ever locked away.

Feature comparison

Compare Notion Mahfouz
File format & ownership Proprietary blocks stored in Notion's own database — pages only exist inside Notion. Plain .md files in a folder you choose — open, edit, or grep them with any tool.
Where it runs Notion's cloud. Requires an account and a connection for full functionality. Nowhere but your computer. No account, no server, no Mahfouz cloud — ever.
Version history 7 days on Free, 30 on Plus, 90 on Business — unlimited only on Enterprise. Full history in a real Git repo, kept as long as you keep it — not a vendor's retention window.
Offline use Limited: you choose individual pages to download; everything else needs a connection. Fully local-first. Works offline by default — there's no "download for offline" step.
Real-time collaboration Built in, inside Notion's hosted workspace. Git-based: share a vault the way you'd share any repo, and everyone works from their own full copy.
Price Free tier is limited; paid plans run from about €9.50/member/month (Plus) up to €19.50 (Business), billed per seat. Free and MIT-licensed, forever. One download, no seats.
Data export / lock-in Full workspace export (HTML/Markdown/CSV) exists but isn't on the Free plan, and it's a one-time snapshot, not how the data normally lives. Nothing to export — your notes were never anything but files.

Notion pricing and plan limits shown as published on notion.com; check their pricing page for current figures.

Questions

Notion, answered.

Can I open my notes without Mahfouz?

Yes — every note is a plain .md file. Open it in any text editor, script against it, or hand it to an AI agent; nothing is trapped in a proprietary format the way Notion's blocks are.

Does Mahfouz have real-time multiplayer editing like Notion?

No — Mahfouz is a local-first, single-writer editor. Collaboration happens through Git: push, pull, and merge like any other repo, rather than everyone typing into the same live document.

What does Mahfouz cost compared to Notion?

Mahfouz is free and MIT-licensed, with no seats to pay for. Notion's paid plans are billed per member per month, and features like full workspace export or unlimited page history sit behind higher tiers.

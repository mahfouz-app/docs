# Moving sections

Put the cursor on a heading below and press Alt+Up or Alt+Down (Option on a Mac) to move its whole section. On a list item, the item moves with its sub-items.

## First section

Text that belongs to the first section.

### A subsection

It moves with the first section.

## Second section

Text that belongs to the second section.

## A long numbered list

Move item 9 down past item 10, and back: its sub-items stay inside it and the numbers stay in order.

1. One
2. Two
3. Three
4. Four
5. Five
6. Six
7. Seven
8. Eight
9. Nine
   - A sub-item of nine
   - Another sub-item of nine
10. Ten
    - A sub-item of ten

## A loose sub-list

Move "Apples" below "Pears": no blank line is added before the outer list.

- Fruit

  - Apples

  - Pears
- Vegetables

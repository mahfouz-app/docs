**Present** any note as a [Slidev](https://sli.dev) deck, in a tab or full
screen (<kbd>Mod</kbd><kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>P</kbd>). Every bare `---` line
is a slide break.

- The vault's slides template (`.config/slides.md`) styles every deck: its
  fonts, colors, logo, header and footer.
- Export a deck as an editable **PowerPoint** file, which Google Slides
  can import.
- It installs its own Node.js, so you don't need one.

See [Presenting notes as slides](/guide/slides/) for slide layouts and
templates.

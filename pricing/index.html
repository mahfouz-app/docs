Questions.

Are taxes included?

No. Sales tax or VAT for where you live is added at checkout, before you pay.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from Settings → Plan → Manage billing. You keep your plan until the period you paid for ends.

What pauses if I move down a plan?

Agent repositories and custom domains over the new limit pause. Nothing is deleted, and upgrading picks them up again.

Who handles payments?

Stripe. It keeps your email and billing address; we never see your card.
